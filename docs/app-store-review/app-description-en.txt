Yummy Go is a restaurant point-of-sale workspace for authorized staff of registered restaurants and cafes.

After signing in, staff can:
- View table availability and open a table
- Create and update dine-in orders
- Browse products and categories, then add product options and notes
- Record payments and prepare receipts
- Review sales, payments, categories, best-selling products, and daily closing reports
- Manage products, tables, customers, users, and branch settings according to their assigned permissions

An active business account is required. Accounts are issued and managed by each restaurant's administrator. Yummy Go is an operational business tool; it is not a consumer food delivery or restaurant discovery app.
